# QA: Panda Jump on Phaser 4, slice 1

Use a desktop browser with dev tools, and a phone or touch emulation for step 9. Heights are in px above the floor surface (y 426).

1. Run `git ls-files main.js phaser.min.js` and `git diff master -- assets/`. **Expected:** both print nothing: the old game is gone and no asset changed.
2. Run `npm ci` then `npm run dev` and open the URL it prints. **Expected:** the page title and heading read "Panda Jump"; below the 400×490 game the page reads "Check it out on Github" (link to https://github.com/maxh213/PandaJump) and "Controls: Click, tap, Space, Up Arrow or controller A to jump (double jump too). P, Escape or Start pauses and resumes.".
2a. In dev tools, inspect the `<html>` element (or run `document.documentElement.lang` in the console). **Expected:** it reads `lang="en"`.
3. Open the Network tab and reload. **Expected:** the Lato font loads over `https://`; no request uses `http://`; nothing requests `main.js` or `phaser.min.js`; the console shows no errors. Find the request for the page's `<link rel="icon">` href. **Expected:** it resolves with status 200, not a 404, points at `icon.png` (not `assets/Panda.png`), and opening it directly shows a square single Panda pose, not the tall, squashed sprite sheet.

3a. View the page source (or inspect `<head>`). **Expected:** a `<meta name="description">` with non-empty, game-specific text; `<meta property="og:type">` reading "website"; `<meta property="og:title">` reading "Panda Jump"; `<meta property="og:description">` with non-empty, game-specific text; `<meta property="og:image">` pointing at `panda-icon.png`, a single-frame square Panda image, not the raw `assets/Panda.png` sprite strip; `<meta property="og:url">` pointing at `https://maxh213.github.io/PandaJump/`; `<meta name="twitter:card">` reading "summary" (the image is a square icon, not a wide banner); `<meta name="twitter:title">` reading "Panda Jump"; `<meta name="twitter:description">` matching the og:description text; `<meta name="twitter:image">` pointing at the same `panda-icon.png`. Paste the page URL into a chat app that shows link previews (e.g. Slack, Discord or iMessage). **Expected:** the preview shows a clean, recognisable panda likeness and the description text instead of a blank card or a stretched smear. Paste the page URL into a preview tool that reads Twitter Card tags (e.g. https://cards-dev.twitter.com/validator or a similar card-preview tool). **Expected:** it renders a summary card with the "Panda Jump" title, the description text and the panda image, instead of a bare title link.
3b. In the page source, find the `<link rel="apple-touch-icon">` href in `<head>` and open it directly in a new tab (or check it in the Network tab if the browser preloads it). **Expected:** it points at `panda-icon.png`, not `assets/Panda.png`, and loads with status 200, not a 404, showing a square single-frame panda. On an iPhone or iPad in Safari, open the dev server URL, tap Share then "Add to Home Screen". **Expected:** the preview and the resulting home screen icon show a clean, recognisable panda likeness, not a stretched slice of the run strip.
3c. In the page source, find `<meta name="mobile-web-app-capable" content="yes">` and `<meta name="apple-mobile-web-app-capable" content="yes">` in `<head>`. **Expected:** both are present with content "yes"; this much is proven automatically in `e2e/`. On an iPhone or iPad in Safari, open the icon added to the home screen in step 3b. **Expected:** the game launches full screen with no Safari address bar or tab bar; a Playwright browser cannot simulate iOS's home-screen launch chrome, so only a real device confirms this.
3d. In the page source, find the two `<link rel="preconnect">` elements in `<head>`. **Expected:** one has `href="https://fonts.googleapis.com"`; the other has `href="https://fonts.gstatic.com"` and a `crossorigin` attribute, so the browser opens the connection to fetch the Lato font from step 3 sooner.
4. Look at the game without touching anything. **Expected:** light blue `#71c5cf` background; score "0" in white at the top left; the panda stands on the floor near the left (left edge at x 100) and plays a looping run animation.
5. Pause the animation in dev tools or record the screen and step through a full run cycle. **Expected:** each of the 6 frames shows the whole panda with no sliver of another pose along its top or bottom edge.
6. Watch the floor for 10 seconds. **Expected:** rock with grass on top scrolls left steadily; no blue gap, seam or flicker between tiles.
7. Wait about 1.5 s after load. **Expected:** a column of 1 or 2 dirt boxes enters from the right edge, sitting on the floor, and moves left at the floor's speed. A new column follows every 1.5 s.
8. Do not jump. **Expected:** when the panda touches the first column the run freezes in place (panda, columns and floor stop moving) and the game shows "Game over" centred in white 40px, with "Score: 0" and "Best: 0" under it, but no restart prompt yet. Click, tap, press Space or press the Up Arrow key right away. **Expected:** nothing happens for about half a second, and the screen still does not invite a tap, click, Space or Up Arrow press. After about half a second, "Tap, press Space or the Up Arrow key to play again" appears in white 16px under the Score and Best lines, fully on screen with no letters cut off at either edge. Click, tap, press Space or press the Up Arrow key again. **Expected:** a fresh run starts at score "0" with no boxes on screen, the panda back at x 100 on the floor, and the game-over screen gone.
8a. Press and hold down the spacebar before the panda reaches the first column, and keep it held down (do not release it) through the panda dying and the "Tap, press Space or the Up Arrow key to play again" prompt appearing, letting the operating system's auto-repeat keep sending keydowns the whole time. **Expected:** the run does not restart on its own; "Game over" and the restart prompt stay on screen no matter how long the key is held. Release the spacebar, then press it once more. **Expected:** the run restarts fresh, with the score at "0".
8b. Open `./?clock=manual&random=0.25,0.5,0` and press Space. In the console run `window.pandaJump.run.advance(2755)`, press Space to jump, then `window.pandaJump.run.advance(127)` and look at the panda: its drawn frame overlaps the column's top-left corner by 3 px or less. **Expected:** no game over; `window.pandaJump.run.advance(500)` still leaves `window.pandaJump.run.view().gameOver` false and the score at 1. Reload with the same URL, press Space, run `window.pandaJump.run.advance(2800)`, press Space, then `window.pandaJump.run.advance(200)`. **Expected:** the game shows "Game over" and the column is tinted red. The panda's hitbox is x 103 to x 122 and forgives its bottom 4 px, so a column of n boxes touches it only while its height is below n * 64 - 4.
9. Tap the canvas on a touch device, click it with a mouse, press Space, and press the Up Arrow key, each with the page focused. Then make the window short (e.g. 800×500) so the page scrolls, scroll to the top and press Space, then repeat scrolled to the top and press the Up Arrow key. **Expected:** each makes the panda jump about 168 px high and land again; neither Space nor the Up Arrow key scrolls the page.
9a. Press and hold down the spacebar instead of tapping it, and keep it held down past the top of the jump. **Expected:** the panda makes a single jump to about 168 px and comes back down; holding the key down (letting the operating system's auto-repeat fire) does not add the smaller second boost that a genuine second press gives in step 10.
10. Jump, then press Space again near the top of the jump. Repeat, jumping and double-jumping with the Up Arrow key instead. **Expected:** each control gets the panda a smaller second boost to about 199 px high.
11. Jump, jump again in the air, then press Space a third time before landing. Repeat with the Up Arrow key instead. **Expected:** the third press does nothing, for either control. After landing, a jump and a double jump both work again.
12. Jump over a one-box column. **Expected:** the score stays at its value while the column is beside or under the panda and goes up by exactly 1 as the column's right edge passes the panda's left edge; it does not rise again for that column, and a column appearing on the right never changes the score.
13. Clear a two-box column using a double jump. **Expected:** the score goes up by exactly 1.
14. Wait for a two-box column. Jump once, with no second jump, when the column's left edge is about 130 px (roughly five panda widths) ahead of the panda's front; anything from about 85 to 170 px works. **Expected:** the panda rises above the column, and the run freezes with the game-over screen as it comes down onto the column's top.
15. Tap to play again after each of 3 deaths in a row, then watch 4 s. **Expected:** after each restart no old box remains, and columns still arrive one every 1.5 s, never two at once from the right edge while the score is 10 or less.
16. Play until the score is above 10 and watch 15 columns. **Expected:** some columns (about a third) arrive with an identical column directly behind them; clearing such a pair adds exactly 1.
17. Stop the dev server. Run `npm run build`, then serve `dist/` from a subfolder, e.g. `mkdir -p /tmp/host/PandaJump && cp -r dist/. /tmp/host/PandaJump && npx serve /tmp/host`, and open `/PandaJump/`. **Expected:** the same page and game as step 4, every asset loads with status 200, and steps 7–9 behave the same. Check the Network tab for the `<link rel="icon">` request. **Expected:** it resolves with status 200 under the `/PandaJump/` prefix too, not a 404, and still points at `icon.png`. Check `dist/icon.png` on disk. **Expected:** it exists (unhashed) so the favicon resolves under this prefix too. Check `dist/panda-icon.png` on disk. **Expected:** it exists (unhashed) so the `<meta property="og:image">` URL from step 3a resolves with status 200 under this prefix too, instead of 404ing the way a hashed filename would. Open the `<link rel="apple-touch-icon">` href directly under that same `/PandaJump/` prefix. **Expected:** it also resolves with status 200, not a 404, and shows the same recognisable panda likeness.
18. Watch the browser tab while you play. **Expected:** the tab reads "Panda Jump" while the score is 0. Clear a column so the score rises above 0. **Expected:** the tab updates to match, e.g. "1 - Panda Jump", then "2 - Panda Jump" as you clear more. Switch to another tab and back. **Expected:** the tab title still shows the current score even while the game tab is in the background. Deliberately die, wait for the restart prompt, and tap to play again. **Expected:** once the fresh run's score reads "0" the tab title returns to exactly "Panda Jump".
19. Run `npx playwright test`. **Expected:** every scenario in `features/phaser-4-core-run.feature` has a passing test.
